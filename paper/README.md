# Papers and Articles

This directory contains public manuscript and article versions associated with this repository.

Planned materials include:

- Research preprint: *Historical Evidence for Off-Policy Evaluation of ETF Allocation Policies: When Better Value Estimates Do Not Resolve Policy Choice*
- Medium article: *When Better Offline Evaluation Still Cannot Choose the Better Policy*

The research paper presents the full experimental protocol, off-policy evaluation methodology, robustness analysis, and limitations.

The Medium article provides a shorter practical interpretation focused on reinforcement learning, contextual bandits, offline policy evaluation, and decision resolution.
